🎯 Price Sniper privacy

Price Sniper is a small, private deal-watching app run by one person for themselves and a few friends. It isn't a company and doesn't sell or share your data.

What's stored

  • Your account: your email and your password, stored only as a scrambled hash that can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your watches and their results: what you search for, your price limits, ZIP code and radius, and the public listings the app found for you.
  • Your settings: ntfy topic, Discord webhook, quiet hours, muted sellers, hidden listings.
  • Gmail (only if you connect it): a read-only access key, stored encrypted. The app only reads marketplace alert emails (Craigslist, OfferUp, …) to find listings. It never sends, deletes, or changes email.

Who can see it

  • Other users can't see your watches, results, alerts, settings, or email.
  • The person who runs the server can technically access the server's files, the same as any self-hosted app.

What's sent to other services

  • Marketplaces (eBay, Best Buy, Reddit, …) get your search words, and for eBay local pickup, your ZIP code and radius. They don't get your name or email.
  • ntfy / Discord get the text of your deal alerts, if you set them up.
  • Google is involved only if you connect Gmail.
  • DuckDuckGo's icon service sends your browser the little store logos (Newegg, Amazon, …). It only sees the store's web address, never what you searched for or who you are.

Listing details such as titles, prices, and seller names come from those marketplaces' public listings and belong to them. Tap Open ↗ to buy on the marketplace itself.

How long it's kept

Listings that are gone are deleted after 60 days, price history after a year, and alerts after 180 days. The server keeps rotating backups for 14 days.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my account removes your account and everything above right away. It disappears from backups as they rotate out within 14 days. You can also disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings, or revoke it from your Google account.
